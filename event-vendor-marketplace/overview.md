# Case Study: Event Management & Vendor Marketplace

## Domain & Problem Overview

Event organizers require reliable discovery across dozens of vendor categories (catering, photography, venues, sound, decor) across different metropolitan cities. Vendors require instant visibility into incoming client inquiries, lead management tools, and media-rich portfolio presentation.

---

## Technical Highlights

- **Multi-Faceted Search Indexing**: Compound index modeling across city, category, price tier, and availability.
- **Media Asset Optimization**: Aggressive CDN caching and responsive image transformation pipelines to handle photo-heavy vendor galleries without degrading mobile page speeds.
- **Real-Time Lead Alerts**: WebSocket channels connected to mobile devices and web portals to ensure instant quotation turnaround.
